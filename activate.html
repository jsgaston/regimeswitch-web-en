Your MT5 account number

Open MetaTrader 5 and look at the Navigator panel, under Accounts. The number next to your broker's name is the login — usually seven to nine digits. It is also the number at the top of the terminal window and in your broker's welcome email. It is not your password and not your investor password, and you should never send either of those to anyone, including me.

  1. Copy your account number

    Demo or live, both work. If you want to test on demo first, send the demo number now and the live one later — transfers are free.

  2. Email it with your payment receipt

    Send both to licenses@wisfli.com. The receipt is whatever Stripe or PayPal sent you; forwarding it is enough.

  3. Wait for the key

    You will get the compiled expert advisor, your key and a one-page setup note. Normally within a few hours, always within one working day.

Two things worth doing now

Download the history

The model trains on the bars your terminal holds. Open a chart for each symbol you plan to run, set the timeframe you want, and scroll back a few years so MetaTrader pulls the history down. Do this once and the warm-up is quick.

Allow algorithmic trading

Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, tick "Allow algorithmic trading". The toolbar button has to be green as well, otherwise the robot reads the market and never places an order.
